How the merger works

No switching apps. No copy-pasting between chats.

One composer sends your prompt to both models at the same time — the same way you'd message one assistant today.

02

Each plays its strength

Meta AI answers fast and socially — trends, images, what's happening. Muse answers personally — your context, your files, your next move.

03

Merged mode picks the best

Flip to Merged and the two answers collapse into one: Meta's speed and reach, Muse's memory and follow-through, no fluff from either.
